Marketplace Safety Tips

Stay safe when buying and selling in your community.

📍

Meet in Public Places

Choose busy, well-lit locations for exchanges. Shopping centers and cafes are good options.

🔍

Inspect Before Paying

Always check the item's condition in person before completing any payment.

💳

Never Pay in Advance

Avoid sending money before seeing the product. Complete transactions in person when possible.

🚨

Report Suspicious Activity

If something feels wrong, trust your instincts and contact our support team immediately.
